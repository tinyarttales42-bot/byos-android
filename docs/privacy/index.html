BYOS — Privacy policy
Updated 27 October 2026

BYOS (Bring Your Own Song) is provided by tinyarttales42. Contact: tinyarttales42@gmail.com.

Your music stays on your device. With your permission, BYOS reads local audio and its metadata (such as title, artist, album and duration) to display your library and play songs. BYOS does not upload your music or library.

Playlists, custom tags and ratings are stored in the app’s private local database. There is no BYOS account, cloud sync, advertising or analytics SDK. BYOS does not collect or share data off your device and does not request internet access.

Android’s media session and notifications display track information and playback controls to the operating system and authorized media controllers, such as connected headphones.

You can revoke audio access in Android settings. Delete playlists and tags within BYOS. To erase all BYOS settings and database records, clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Your original audio files are not deleted by those actions. Android backup is disabled for BYOS data.

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are handled by the email provider and used to respond to your request. Do not send music files or sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us; messages are retained only as needed to resolve your request or meet legal obligations.

Google Play and Android may process store, installation or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. This policy covers BYOS itself. Future changes to BYOS data handling will be reflected in an updated policy.
